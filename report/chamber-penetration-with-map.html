Also true = chamber members ÷ every business in the county (MBCIA's count), churches and government included on both sides — same members, just a bigger, looser denominator. Not the headline: JobsEQ's establishment count (4,732) already excludes most churches and government the same way this page's numerator does, an apples-to-apples match. Esri's total (6,713) doesn't make that cut and also folds in freelancers, home-based, and other looser listings, inflating the denominator without a matching adjustment on the numerator side.
